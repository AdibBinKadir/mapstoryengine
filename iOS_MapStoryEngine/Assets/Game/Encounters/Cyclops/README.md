# Cyclops encounter

Open `Assets/Game/Sandbox/Cyclops.unity` and enter Play mode. Press 2 twice to simulate two GPS fixes 15 m from the cave. Press 1 to move outside the radius, 3 for poor accuracy, or 4 to expire the current fix. Hold Shift to block and click the boss during recovery.

For a quick phone test, choose **Test Cyclops** from the title screen. For the real-world encounter, open the map and look for the **Cyclops Cave** pin. The event chooses a nearby public landmark from the existing Places results while keeping its entry radius separate from story-stop arrival zones. Enter Cave appears after two fresh fixes within 25 m. The sandbox controls are editor only.

The Cyclops alternates a heavy slam and a faster swipe. Raise the shield when his eye flashes gold to earn a stronger first counter and more time to attack. Tap the boss during the green recovery window.

The fight uses local health and a local result while the shared game and clue contracts are pending.
